# Your music goes here

Drop `.mp3` or `.wav` files into this folder and they show up in SlimCity's
music player (Options → Audio → Music, or the now-playing chip in the status
strip). Hit **Rescan** in the panel and new files appear — no restart needed.

You can also drag files straight onto the game window. Those play for the
current session only; put them here to keep them.

Nothing in this folder is committed to the repository — the audio formats are
git-ignored, so your music stays yours. The game ships no music of its own.
