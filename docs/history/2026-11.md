### A placed building stands beside a street, and a refusal says why (built 2026-11-10)

The player's words: "buildings must be placed next to the road and/or rail
tracks, not distances from it, or just rail and no road, this is wrong."

- **The rule** `[R-world-049]`. The worker refuses a building whose footprint
  touches no street at grade, by one predicate (`touchesStreet`) the ghost
  uses too. A kind that needs track, tram track or a shore needs the street
  as well. Sandbox does not lift it.
- **Four exempt, by data.** The wind turbine, the solar farm, the pumping
  station and the drain carry `offStreet` in the catalog and stand where a
  power line or a pipe reaches them. The advisor no longer counts them as
  cut off from the road network.
- **A refusal is a sentence.** Every way a placement can fail has its own:
  off the map, in water, too steep, occupied, wanting a street, a railway, a
  tram track or a shore. The cursor chip shows the same sentence as the
  toast. A refused park on a slope used to say only "That cannot be built
  there."
- **Old saves keep what they hold.** A building standing off the street
  stays, and undoing its demolition puts it back.
- **Tests and tools moved to the rule.** Tests that placed off the street
  gained a street; the town fixture's sewage works gained one; eight shot
  tools failed on the new refusal, as the guard is meant to make them, and
  had their layouts fixed.

Turning a building's front to its street `[R-world-050]` is the next slice.

### A plot lies flush with its ground and its street (built 2026-11-10)

The player's words: "the base of buildings is higher than the ground… the
green edges should be underground or flush with the ground." A civic plot
showed a step all round, and on a slope a wall a metre high at its own
drive. Two causes, one in the kits and one in the sim.

- **The yard and the pad are ground** `[R-render-034]`. A kit's yard stood
  0.3 m proud of a pad itself 0.25 m proud. Both now top out in the band the
  zoned lots use, 0.08 to 0.12 m over the terrain, from one module,
  `src/render/plotground.ts`; walls, stalls, cars, lamps and fences follow
  it. A test walks every kit's level faces and fails on raised ground.
- **A placed building is levelled to its street** `[R-world-051]`. The sim
  levelled a footprint to the mean of its own ground, so on a lot rising
  behind the street the plot stood above the footway. It now takes the
  highest street edge it touches.
- **The ring is levelled too.** A map height is a cell's, and a drawn corner
  is the mean of the four cells meeting there, so a footprint levelled alone
  still tilted along its north and west edges and the kit seated itself on
  the highest corner. The open ground one tile round a placed building is
  levelled with it; the bank now stands outside the plot.

Left: a pond on a sloped park stands proud on its downhill side, and a lot
a zone grows on still levels without the ring.

### The page keeps answering while a city grows at 4× (built 2026-11-10)

With a hundred blocks zoned at once and the speed at 4×, the page stopped
answering for a minute at a time and then ran out of memory. A CPU profile
showed why: the worker posted 53 snapshots a second, and each did work over
the whole city on the main thread.

- **A snapshot costs what it changed** `[R-render-037]`. The tiles a lot's
  drive crosses and each walker's path are kept per building and laid again
  only for the buildings a delta names, or for all when a road, a section or
  a junction control moves.
- **Pools start large.** Below about a thousand instances three compiles an
  instanced mesh's capacity into its shader, so every pool that doubled as
  the city grew linked a new program in the render loop: 280 in one run. No
  pool is now allocated for fewer than 2,048, and 64 first compiles remain.
- **Meshes are kept, not rebuilt.** Pedestrians, median trees, lamps and road
  furniture keep their meshes and set a live count.
- **The leak.** Ten renderers took a replaced mesh off the scene and never
  disposed it; the median trees did so on every junction change. That was the
  memory.
- **One snapshot a pump.** A pump of five ticks posts once, after its last
  tick: 20 snapshots a second at 4×, and nothing is lost, since every delta
  gathers in the worker until a post drains it.
- **Cheaper rebuilds.** A road chunk's forty lookups a tile are one index
  each; a lot's normals are laid once with the lot.
- **The gauge.** `tools/services-shots.mjs` grows its city at 4× again and
  fails on a read-back over its limit, on any page error and on one that
  never returns.

- **Vehicles glide.** They are interpolated over the measured time between
  snapshots, not a fixed tenth of a second: at 1× a car no longer moves for
  100 ms and stands for 200.

The longest read-back fell from 57 s to 1.1 s and the heap from 2.1 GB to
0.6 GB, against the 1 s target in
[performance-budget.md](../engineering/performance-budget.md#the-main-thread-while-a-city-grows).

  [defects list](../roadmap/open-visual-defects.md): a door that always

  is whole.

### Every shot reviewed, and the tools that hid a refusal fixed (built 2026-11-10)

At the player's request every screenshot the shot tools write was looked at
for what is wrong in the game: 741 frames in 46 folders, then the re-shot
frames again. What it found is ranked in
[open-visual-defects.md](../roadmap/open-visual-defects.md). It also found
most of the tools themselves stale: the game had refused a command and the
tool went on shooting, so a folder showed a failure toast over an empty
field.

- **A refused command fails the tool.** `cleanShot` in `tools/shotcam.mjs`
  reads the toasts before each frame, stops on one titled "… failed", clears
  the rest and names what it cleared, and prints the advisor's alerts once a
  run. A tool that means to show a refusal says which one it allows.
- **Stale towns laid to today's rules.** A water tower touches its road; a
  bus stop is street furniture and its line has a powered depot; a rail
  station has a street to open onto; a highway is met only by a ramp; a town
  past 3,500 people has its sewer; a plant and a tower reach the streets they
  serve.
- **No false alarm in frame.** A tool town built with unlimited money says
  so to the interface as well as to the simulation (`unlimitedMoneyOption`),
  so no shot carries a red treasury and a "city is in the red" badge.
- **Frames aimed at their subject.** Close shots frame the building, not the
  street row; the bottom bar no longer hides a lot's street edge; lenses and
  panels stay off the subject; night shots are night; duplicates are gone; a
  train, a tram and a bus are in frame.
- **Two tools fail on purpose.** `nightlook-shots` reads a roof near black at
  noon and `corridor-shots` finds a turn bay drawn on the side its lanes
  leave by: both are defects in the game, on the list, and the tools keep
  failing until they are fixed.
- **Five rules the player stated**, written down and not yet built: a kit's
  plot is flush with its ground `[R-render-034]`, a placed building stands
  beside a street `[R-world-049]` with its front turned to it
  `[R-world-050]`, a fence keeps to its own lot's line `[R-render-035]`, and
  a bend is an arc `[R-roads-081]`.
- **Three specs agreed**, not yet built:
  [power distribution](../specs/power-distribution.md) `[R-services-039]`,
  [structured parking](../specs/structured-parking.md) `[R-services-040]`
  and [street furniture](../specs/street-furniture.md) `[R-render-036]`.
