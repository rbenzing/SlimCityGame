### Every shot reviewed, and the tools that hid a refusal fixed (built 2026-11-10)

At the player's request every screenshot the shot tools write was looked at
for what is wrong in the game: 741 frames in 46 folders, then the re-shot
frames again. What it found is ranked in
[open-visual-defects.md](../roadmap/open-visual-defects.md). It also found
most of the tools themselves stale: the game had refused a command and the
tool went on shooting, so a folder showed a failure toast over an empty
field.

- **A refused command fails the tool.** `cleanShot` in `tools/shotcam.mjs`
  reads the toasts before each frame, stops on one titled "… failed", clears
  the rest and names what it cleared, and prints the advisor's alerts once a
  run. A tool that means to show a refusal says which one it allows.
- **Stale towns laid to today's rules.** A water tower touches its road; a
  bus stop is street furniture and its line has a powered depot; a rail
  station has a street to open onto; a highway is met only by a ramp; a town
  past 3,500 people has its sewer; a plant and a tower reach the streets they
  serve.
- **No false alarm in frame.** A tool town built with unlimited money says
  so to the interface as well as to the simulation (`unlimitedMoneyOption`),
  so no shot carries a red treasury and a "city is in the red" badge.
- **Frames aimed at their subject.** Close shots frame the building, not the
  street row; the bottom bar no longer hides a lot's street edge; lenses and
  panels stay off the subject; night shots are night; duplicates are gone; a
  train, a tram and a bus are in frame.
- **Two tools fail on purpose.** `nightlook-shots` reads a roof near black at
  noon and `corridor-shots` finds a turn bay drawn on the side its lanes
  leave by: both are defects in the game, on the list, and the tools keep
  failing until they are fixed.
- **Five rules the player stated**, written down and not yet built: a kit's
  plot is flush with its ground `[R-render-034]`, a placed building stands
  beside a street `[R-world-049]` with its front turned to it
  `[R-world-050]`, a fence keeps to its own lot's line `[R-render-035]`, and
  a bend is an arc `[R-roads-081]`.
- **Three specs agreed**, not yet built:
  [power distribution](../specs/power-distribution.md) `[R-services-039]`,
  [structured parking](../specs/structured-parking.md) `[R-services-040]`
  and [street furniture](../specs/street-furniture.md) `[R-render-036]`.
