# 2. Design pillars (what "feels like a city builder" actually means)

The six pillars are stated once, in the game design document:
[gdd.md](../game-design/gdd.md). In short: zones are painted, not houses
placed; roads are the skeleton; the city is read through data lenses; the
city looks alive; the interface uses the city-builder grammar; and every
system doubles as an engine showcase that stays smooth in a browser tab.
