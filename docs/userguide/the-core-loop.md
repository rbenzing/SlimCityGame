# The core loop

1. **Roads are the skeleton.** Everything needs road access. Lay roads first;
   the buildable grid appears alongside them.
2. **Paint zones, don't build houses.** Mark land residential, commercial, or
   industrial. Demand (the RCI bars) decides whether and how fast it fills in.
   Until the town is a Grand City, shops and works take roomy lots with a
   car park, planting and a yard, so give them more land than their
   buildings alone would need; a block with no room still grows tight.
   How tall a town builds follows its size: nothing over four storeys before
   Small City, six storeys there, twelve at Grand City, and the tallest
   towers only at Metropolis. Nothing standing is torn down for being too
   tall. A building that upgrades is rebuilt larger: on its own lot where
   the bigger building and its car park fit, onto free ground beside it
   where they do not. From Big Town a shop, restaurant, office, hotel or
   apartment block with no room to spread is rebuilt taller on the lot it
   has, with its parking inside; leave ground free beside a building you
   would rather see spread.
3. **Power and water flow through roads.** Connect a generator and a water source
   to the road network and the whole connected network is served.
4. **Watch it grow, then read it.** Buildings construct, upgrade, and occasionally
   abandon on their own. Use the infoview lenses and stats to see why. An
   abandoned building reopens when its power, water and road return;
   otherwise it stands boarded up until its zone is in demand again, or
   until you bulldoze it.
5. **Hit milestones.** Growth earns milestone progress, which unlocks denser
   zones, bigger roads, and more services.
