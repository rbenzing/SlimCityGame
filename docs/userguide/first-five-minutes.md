# First five minutes

1. Draw a **two-lane road** out into the map (Roads category, or press `1`).
2. Place a **power source** (a wind turbine to start), a **water source** (a
   water tower) and a **drain** (a water drain pipe, on a shore). **The water
   tower must stand directly next to a road**: the game refuses a building
   placed off the street and says why. A turbine and a drain may stand off
   it, but only a road, a power line or a pipe laid to them carries their
   power or sewage, and without that nothing will grow.
3. **Paint a residential zone** (Zoning, or `2`) in a band along the road, then a
   little **commercial** and **industry** so residents have shops and jobs.
4. Unpause (`Space`) and let it run. Keep an eye on the RCI demand bars and your
   money.
