# Street furniture placement: sources

Research for [street-furniture.md](../specs/street-furniture.md). _Read_ marks a page that was opened; _relayed_ marks a figure taken from a search result's summary, to be confirmed before it is leaned on.

## The strip and the walk

- A sidewalk has a frontage zone (18 in; 2 ft on commercial streets), a throughway (6 ft on most streets, 5 ft in alleys, 4 ft at the least) kept "clear of obstacles" with 80 in of headroom, and a furnishing zone (3 ft with trees, 4 ft with no parking lane) where poles, lights, hydrants, trees, benches and utility boxes are "consolidated". Poles, meters and tree pits may stand within 18 in of the kerb where car doors still open. [San Francisco Better Streets](https://sfbetterstreets.org/design-guidelines/sidewalk-zones). _Read._
- An obstruction is at least 1.5 ft from the kerb face (attributed to AASHTO in a [Mohave County lighting directive](https://www.mohave.gov/departments/public-works/documents/roadway-lighting-design-directive/)). _Relayed._ The game's bus-stop pole already uses 18 in, in [the bus-stop sources](civic-access-and-bus-stops.md).
- "The clear width of walking surfaces shall be 36 inches (915 mm) minimum." [ADA Standards 403.5.1](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch04/). _Read._
- The public right-of-way guidelines ask 48 in of continuous clear width, which an obstruction may not reduce (R302.2, [US Access Board](https://www.access-board.gov/prowag/technical.html)). _Relayed._

## Bus stops

- A boarding and alighting area has "a clear length of 96 inches (2440 mm) minimum, measured perpendicular to the curb or vehicle roadway edge, and a clear width of 60 inches (1525 mm) minimum, measured parallel to the vehicle roadway." [ADA Standards 810.2.2](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch08/). _Read._
- A clear path of 5 ft past a shelter, 4 ft at the least ([Los Angeles](https://engpermitmanual.lacity.org/sites/default/files/documents/4-15-19_Clearance%20Requirements%20for%20Street%20Furniture.pdf)); a shelter 15 ft from hydrants and utility poles ([Apex, North Carolina](https://www.apexnc.org/DocumentCenter/View/39144)). _Relayed._

## Street lights

- Streets "50 feet wide or less may have street lighting in an alternating pattern spaced every 180 feet"; wider streets have lights "placed opposite each other and spaced every 250 feet". Lights at a junction stand downstream of the curb ramps and "at least 10 feet" from a crosswalk. Pedestrian lights "mount 12 to 14 feet above the sidewalk". [Seattle Streets Illustrated](https://streetsillustrated.seattle.gov/design-standards/lighting/). _Read._
- Pole heights of 20, 30 and 40 ft by road width ([Sioux Falls](https://www.siouxfalls.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/planning-and-development/construction-mgmt-amp-design/engineering-design-standards/u30015_roadway-lighting-23.pdf)); spacing 250 ft desired and 300 ft at most ([Liberty, Missouri](https://www.libertymissouri.gov/DocumentCenter/View/27343/Street-Lighting-Design-Criteria)). _Relayed._
- One side for one to three lanes, staggered for three to six, opposite for five and more (the FHWA Lighting Handbook, as summarised). _Relayed; the page refused the fetch._
- Behind a barrier kerb at 45 mph or less a pole stands 2.5 ft back at the least, 10 ft by preference. [TxDOT pole placement](https://www.txdot.gov/manuals/trf/hwi/lighting_design_and_layout/pole_placement_guidelines-chdcgcic/breakaway_pole_placement_examples-i1005153.html). _Read._
- Not found: spacing as a multiple of mounting height; a pole's distance from a drive, a hydrant or a kerb ramp.

## Roads with no kerb

- With no kerb a pole stands 10 ft from the travel lane at 40 mph or less (15 ft by preference) and 15 ft at 45 mph or more (20 ft by preference), or "as near the right-of-way line as practicable". [TxDOT, same page](https://www.txdot.gov/manuals/trf/hwi/lighting_design_and_layout/pole_placement_guidelines-chdcgcic/breakaway_pole_placement_examples-i1005153.html). _Read._
- Clear zone at 40 mph or less: 10 to 16 ft preferred by traffic volume, 7 to 14 ft acceptable; at 45 to 50 mph, 12 to 22 ft preferred. [Iowa DOT, from the AASHTO Roadside Design Guide](https://iowadot.gov/design-manual/chapter-8-roadside-safety/8a-2-clear-zones). _Read._

## Signs and signals

- A sign in a built-up area may stand "at least 1 foot from the face of the curb"; elsewhere 12 ft from the travelled way, 2 ft where that is impractical (MUTCD 2A.19). Its bottom is 7 ft above the kerb top where people walk or park, 5 ft above the pavement edge in the country (2A.18). [MUTCD Part 2A](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/htm/2009/part2/part2a.htm). _Read._
- A STOP sign stands on the near side, on the right, "as close as practical to the intersection it regulates", no more than 50 ft from the crossed road's edge (2B.10). [MUTCD Part 2B](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/htm/2009/part2/part2b.htm). _Read._
- A signal face under 15 ft high has "a horizontal offset of not less than 2 feet from the face of a vertical curb" (4D.16); a head over the road is 15 ft to 25.6 ft above the pavement (4D.15). [MUTCD Part 4D](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/htm/2009/part4/part4d.htm). _Read._
- A push-button is about 3.5 ft high, 1.5 to 6 ft from the kerb and within 5 ft of the crosswalk line (4E.08). [MUTCD Part 4E](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/htm/2009/part4/part4e.htm). _Read._
- Where the mast stands differs by city: 2 ft off the kerb face in Minneapolis and Liberty, the pole's centre 6 ft back in Gardner, Kansas; a controller cabinet behind the sidewalk, out of sight lines. _Relayed._

## Covers

- "Catch basins and manholes shall be located outside of travel lanes or normal wheel paths" ([MassDOT](https://www.mass.gov/doc/manholes-and-catch-basins/download)); "Manholes shall not be located in a wheel path" ([Arkansas](https://www.state-rules.com/arkansas/car/91-303/)); covers "outside the travel path of bicyclists" (a Minnesota bikeways guide). _Relayed._ One city asks for the centre line instead ([Coeur d'Alene](https://www.cdaid.org/files/Wastewater/Policy_713.pdf)); the game takes the lane's middle, which is off the paint.

## Street trees

## Not in the game

Hydrants stand 2 ft behind the kerb face, 500 ft apart on residential streets and 300 ft elsewhere, with no parking for 15 ft either side (local codes, _relayed_). The game draws none, nor benches or mailboxes, and the spec proposes none.
