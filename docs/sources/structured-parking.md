# Structured parking: sources

Research for [structured-parking.md](../specs/structured-parking.md). _Read_ marks a page that was opened; _relayed_ marks a figure taken from a search result's summary of the page, to be confirmed before it is leaned on.

## What a building needs

- General office, 2.98 spaces per 1,000 sq ft at the 85th percentile (ITE Parking Generation, land use 710), as quoted in a [Fairfax, Virginia study](https://fairfaxva.prod.govaccess.org/home/showpublisheddocument/24455/638693327842730000). _Relayed._ The game's 3 is the modal code minimum in [parking-to-code.md](parking-to-code.md).
- A survey of code minimums for offices averaged 3.7 per 1,000 sq ft, a third above the ITE rate ([Shoup](https://escholarship.org/content/qt4vz087cc/qt4vz087cc.pdf)). _Relayed._
- Apartments (ITE 221): average demand 1.34 a home less 8.73, 85th percentile 1.47 a home, in a [Plantation, Florida document](https://plantation.org/home/showpublisheddocument/4766/637771557070770000). _Relayed._ The walk-up's 1.4 sits between them.
- Hotel: zoning minimums of 1.0 a room with additions for staff and meeting rooms ([an example table](https://ecode360.com/8800861)); ITE demand 0.74 a key in a [Newton, Massachusetts plan](https://newtonma.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/49743/637429497878400000). _Relayed._
- Not found: a room count for the game's hotels. The 45 m² of floor a room is a dial.

## Downtown

- "No parking shall be required within the Manhattan Core." [New York Zoning Resolution 13-10](https://zoningresolution.planning.nyc.gov/article-i/chapter-3). _Read._
- Austin requires none in its central business district and downtown mixed-use district (Land Development Code 25-6-591(B), as [summarised by WGI](https://wginc.com/are-there-any-parking-reductions-or-credits-in-the-city-of-austin/)); Urbana, Illinois none in its B-4 district ([Parking Reform Network](https://parkingreform.org/mandates-map/city_detail/Urbana_IL.html)); Quincy, Illinois halves it within 600 ft of a municipal lot ([code](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/quincy/latest/quincy_il/0-0-0-12297)). _Relayed._
- What downtown offices provide: about 1 stall per 1,000 sq ft against up to 4 in the suburbs, a broker quoted in the [San Diego Business Journal](https://sdbj.com/imported/cheap-downtown-parking-rates-cause-sticker-shock/). _Relayed; one source, undated._
- Downtown hotels: six Los Angeles comparables at 0.2 to 0.9 stalls a room, 0.5 weighted. _Relayed; the memo's address was not kept._
- Apartments at a dense site near transit: 0.71 a home, range 0.61 to 0.81 (ITE 221, in a [Vail, Colorado report](https://vail.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/1852/637552054900030000)). _Relayed._
- Not found: the density, floor ratio or land price at which builders move from a lot to a deck, beyond one figure that structure pays where land passes about $1 million an acre ([VTPI](https://vtpi.org/tca/tca0504.pdf), _relayed_).

## The garage

- Accessible spaces: car 96 in, van 132 in, access aisle 60 in, 98 in clear on a van's route; counts by the table in section 208.2, one in six a van. [US Access Board, chapter 5](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch05/), [chapter 2](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/chapter/ch02/). _Read._
- Bay 36 × 60 ft, three 12 ft double tees; stalls at 90° the most efficient ([Wells, a precast maker](https://wells.build/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Wells-Parking-Structure-Design-Details.pdf)). _Relayed._
- Gross floor a stall: 300 to 350 sq ft in a stand-alone garage; 275 to 350 at a 60 ft clear span ([course notes](https://www.pdhonline.com/courses/g202/g202content.pdf)). _Relayed._
- A floor that is parked on slopes 5 % by preference and never more than 1 in 15, 6.67 % ([Boise guidelines, excerpted](https://www.cityoflfp.gov/DocumentCenter/View/7697/Excerpts-from-Boise-Parking-Structure-Design-Guidelines)); a ramp inside a building 15 % at most in [Evanston, Illinois](https://cityofevanston.org/home/showpublisheddocument/2134/636164694930370000). _Relayed._
- Floor to floor 10.5 to 11.5 ft. _Relayed, source not kept; the game's 3.35 m is the hospital deck's and falls inside it._
- Clear height 7 ft; a vehicle barrier 2 ft 9 in where the drop is over 1 ft (the building code's sections 406.4.1 and 406.4.3, as [New York reproduces them](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin/0-0-0-166934)). _Relayed._
- An open garage has openings of at least 20 % of each tier's wall area, along at least 40 % of the tier's perimeter, on two sides or more ([New York's text](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/newyorkcity/latest/NYCadmin/0-0-0-166967)); the 40 % is also in [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multistorey_car_park). _Relayed; read._
- Ramps outside the floor "may take the form of a circular or helical ramp". [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multistorey_car_park). _Read._
- Not found, so drawn from the look of the type and marked as dials: where the stair and lift towers stand, the entry lanes and gate, roof lighting, the stalls a tier of a small-city garage holds.

## Built into a building

- "5-over-1": four or five timber storeys over one concrete podium; "5-over-2" over two. A wrap puts the flats round a garage in the middle. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-over-1). _Read._
- A garage's street face lined with buildings over at least 70 % of its length ([Chico, California](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/chico/latest/chico_ca/0-0-0-18426)); the lining floor at least 15 ft deep ([New York](https://zr.planning.nyc.gov/print/pdf/node/17817)). _Relayed._
- Not found: a podium's height, the tiers a tower's podium holds, tiers below ground.

## What it costs

Context only; the game charges nothing for parking.

- A parking structure costs a median $33,300 a space, $98.75 a square foot ([WGI's cost outlook](https://wginc.com/webinar-recap-2026-parking-cost-outlook/)). _Read._
- Above ground $52,000 and below ground $73,000 a stall in hard cost across seventeen cities ([GlobeSt, on a UCLA analysis](https://www.globest.com/amp/2026/03/26/parking-costs-erode-yields-as-stall-prices-climb-above-70k/)). _Relayed._
