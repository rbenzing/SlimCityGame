# Civic access and bus stops: sources and derivations

The research behind [the civic-access-and-bus-stops spec](../specs/civic-access-and-bus-stops.md): the published standards for a bus stop and a civic driveway, and what each lands on the tile.

## The boarding pad

The boarding pad is 96 in measured out from the kerb face by 60 in along it, firm and level, joined to the footway ([ADA Standards 810.2](https://www.access-board.gov/ada/), [Arlington VA](https://www.arlingtonva.us/Government/Programs/Transportation/Vision-Zero/Action/Tools-and-Guidelines/Tools/Transit-Stops)).

The game's pad is **2.44 m deep from the kerb face by 3.66 m along it**, the shelter's length, so the shelter stands on it.

A pole and every other vertical element keeps **18 in, 0.46 m, off the kerb face** ([AASHTO via Lane Transit District](https://www.ltd.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Attachment-B-Exhibit-E-2022-06-01_Bus-Stop-Standards_Final.pdf)).

A shelter keeps a **3 ft, 0.9 m, path between it and the kerb** and stands at the back of the footway ([NYC Street Design Manual](https://www.nycstreetdesign.info/index.php/node/143)).

On a road with no footway an agency pours the same pad at the shoulder and a stub of walk to it ([Montgomery County MD](https://www.masstransitmag.com/bus/press-release/11499151/montgomery-county-maryland-md-bus-stop-improvement-program-has-improved-safety-and-accessibility-at-nearly-3000-stops), [Raleigh UDO 8.11.3](https://udo.raleighnc.gov/sec-8113-determining-required-transit-infrastructure)). Grass or gravel never counts as a pad ([Omnitrans](https://omnitrans.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Appendix-E-F-ADA-Compliance-Checklist-and-Stop-Gallery.pdf)).

## Where a stop may stand

A stop stands clear of a junction. A 40 ft bus needs 90 ft of kerb on the far side of a crossing or 100 ft on the near side ([LA Metro](https://busstophub.metro.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Metro_Bus-Stop-Hub_Street-side-Factors.pdf), [NACTO](https://nacto.org/publication/urban-street-design-guide/street-design-elements/transit-streets/bus-stops/)).

So on the tile **a stop never stands on a junction tile**; the tile beside one is the far-side stop the manuals prefer.

Stops are typically 750 ft apart on an urban street and 1,000 ft in a suburb ([TCRP Report 19](https://www.njtpa.org/NJTPA/media/Documents/Planning/Regional-Programs/Studies/Pedestrian%20Safety%20at%20and%20Near%20Bus%20Stops%20Study/Bus-Stop-Safety-Tool-Box_final13.pdf)). The player chooses; the game does not space them.

A stop never stands on a motorway or a ramp, on rail, or on a tram reservation. A tram stop is the tramway's own.

## Sign against shelter

A sign, pole and timetable case cost $296 with $126 to install ([UTA 2023](https://buyapass.rideuta.com/-/media/Files/About-UTA/Reports/2023_BSMP_Compressed_a.pdf)).

A shelter costs $30,000–40,000 bought and installed and $4,000–7,000 a year to clean, repair and clear ([MBTA](https://commonwealthbeacon.org/transportation/t-notes-bus-shelter-maintenance-pricey/), [Sarasota](https://www.yourobserver.com/news/2013/jan/31/scat-pitches-strategic-plan/)).

The shelter keeps its present price, ₵150 and ₵10 a month, as the anchor. The sign is priced by the real ratio, 1 to 83, at ₵2 and nothing a month.

Shelter advertising pays a city a flat $100–300 a shelter a month or 15–35% of the gross ([Pasadena $300](https://ww2.cityofpasadena.net/2015%20Agendas/Aug_17_15/AR%209.pdf), [Burbank 20% with a $100 floor](https://web2.burbankca.gov/cityclerk/agendas/ag_council/2003/sr021103_10.html), [Maryland MTA 35%](https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/2005rs/fnotes/bil_0008/sb0158.pdf)), against $417 a month of upkeep. The game pays ₵7 of the ₵10, the same seven tenths.

Agencies warrant a shelter at **25 boardings a day** in a suburb and 40 downtown ([Metro Transit](https://www.metrotransit.org/Data/Sites/1/media/about/improvements/shelters/shelter-placement-guidelines-update-november-2017.pdf), [Spokane Transit](https://spokanetransit.com/files/projects-plans/PassengerFacilities_Report_4thEd_REV.PDF)). The game takes 25, so an ad panel sells where a shelter was warranted.

## The civic drive

A two-way commercial or civic driveway is 24 ft wide at least and 30–36 ft at most ([Hurricane UT](https://www.zoneomics.com/code/hurricane-UT/chapter_25), [Genesee County MI](https://www.gcrc.org/forms-documents/forms/gcrc-driveway-policies/169-rules-governing-the-granting-of-permits-for-driveways-in-the-genesee-county-road-system/file), [Olathe KS](https://olathe.municipal.codes/UDO/18.30.050)). The game's is **7.3 m**.

A fire apparatus road is 20 ft clear and a two-way fire lane 24 ft ([NFPA 1141 via Santa Clara County Fire](https://sccfd.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/A-1-Fire-Department-Apparatus-Access-05.12.2021.pdf)).

A station's front apron is 45–50 ft deep from the bay doors ([Arlington VA Fire Station 8](https://arlingtonva.us/wp-content/uploads/sites/31/2016/02/FS8-Proposed-Program-020316.pdf), [Fire Engineering](https://www.fireengineering.com/firefighting/from-the-ground-up-constructing-or-reconstructing-a-modern-firehouse-2/)). The game's is **14 m**. Its staff park at the rear or side with a visitor lot by the door ([Fire Apparatus](https://www.fireapparatusmagazine.com/the-fire-station/the-station-articles/five-station-tips/)).

A school bus loop is a 24 ft lane on a 50 ft radius, one way counter-clockwise ([Montgomery County MD](https://www.montgomerycountymd.gov/OLO/Resources/Files/clarksburg/h140.pdf), [California Department of Education](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ls/fa/documents/busdrvwy.pdf)). The game's loop radius is 15 m.

Codes put a civic car park beside or behind the building ([Mocksville NC](https://www.zoneomics.com/code/mocksville-NC/chapter_19), [Milpitas CA](https://www.milpitas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/1198)).
